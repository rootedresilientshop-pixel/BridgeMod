## FAQ for Sponsors

### "Is the SDK free forever?"
**Yes.** The Core SDK (BridgeMod.SDK on NuGet) is MIT-licensed and will always be free. Sponsorships fund advanced features and commercial tooling.

### "What if I sponsor and then stop?"
No problem. You keep the benefits you've accessed (early access, consulting hours, etc.). You'll lose ongoing perks like priority support and design input.

### "How does this relate to the commercial license?"
Sponsorship gets you **early access** to tools. The **commercial license** (Phase 6) will be a separate purchase for studios bundling BridgeMod with proprietary tech. Sponsors get pricing discounts.

### "Can I sponsor part-time?"
Absolutely. GitHub Sponsors pauses/resumes with no penalty. Sponsor for the months you need it.

### "Who do I contact for enterprise licensing?"
Email or open a discussion in GitHub Discussions. We work with large studios on custom arrangements.
